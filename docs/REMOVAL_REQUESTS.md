# Removal requests

This corpus contains derived data from the enacted law of sovereign Tribal Nations. Public
availability is not consent to redistribution. A Nation whose material appears here may have it
removed.

## Who may request removal

Any person authorized to act for the Nation: an elected official, a tribal attorney or
attorney general's office, a tribal court, a records or IT office, or anyone the Nation
designates. We do not require proof of authority beyond a request that comes from a Nation's
own contact channel, and we do not require a reason.

## What to send

The name of the Nation, and whether the request covers all of its material or a specific
document. Nothing further is needed. While this release is anonymized for peer review,
requests go through an issue on the repository. A durable contact address replaces that
channel in the de-anonymized release, and this document is updated with it at that point.
